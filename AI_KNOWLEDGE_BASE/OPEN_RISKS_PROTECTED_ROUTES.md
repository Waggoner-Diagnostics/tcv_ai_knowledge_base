# Open risks - `fix/protected-routes-03nov` (2026-11-04)

Items left open for a **product decision**, or unverified, from the protected-routes work (backend
`fix/protected-routes-03nov`, frontend `fix/protected-routes-03nov`; both unmerged). Nothing here was
changed to "fix" it. Owner column is who must decide. Context: [AUTHORIZATION](AUTHORIZATION.md),
[SECURITY S-22](SECURITY.md#s-22--any-signed-in-account-can-promote-itself-to-super-admin-through-put-apiusersid),
[FRONTEND](FRONTEND.md), [CHANGE_IMPACT_GUIDE](CHANGE_IMPACT_GUIDE.md).

The original defect: once logged in as a Customer or Organization user, every Super Admin route was
reachable in the SPA and every `api/users*` endpoint answered. The frontend route trim closes the first;
the backend `UserController` / `UserRequest` gate closes the second. The backend gate is the one that can break a caller, so it is listed first.
